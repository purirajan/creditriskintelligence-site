How it works

Application data in, decision package out

  1. 1

    Application data

    From your checkout or origination system, by API or batch file

  2. 2

    Policy rules

    Your hard limits first: credit score floor, debt-to-income, missed payments, income verification

  3. 3

    Risk scoring

    12-month probability of default, loss if default, exposure and expected loss

  4. 4

    Decision package

    Approve, review or decline, reason codes and a decision record, in one response

↺ Decisions and repayment outcomes feed portfolio monitoring: approval and review rates by risk grade, and alerts when a segment drifts from expected loss.

Why expected loss

A score tells you who is risky. Expected loss tells you what the risk costs.

PD · probability of default
How likely this applicant is to stop paying within 12 months.
LGD · loss given default
If they stop, the share of the money you don't get back.
EAD · exposure at default
How much they will owe at that moment.
ECL · expected credit loss
The three multiplied: the dollars you should expect to lose on this order.

An anonymised loan tape: application fields, decisions and repayment outcomes. No names, Social Security numbers, addresses or contact details.

Where it runs

In our environment for a backtest, or inside your own cloud as a container if you prefer data never to leave it.

Where we are today

We are an early-stage company and do not yet hold a SOC 2 report. Reason codes are designed to support ECOA / Regulation B adverse-action notices; your compliance team remains the decision-maker.
