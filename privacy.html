Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information CreditDrishti collects through this website (creditdrishti.com) and how it is used. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through our two forms:

  • Backtest request form: your name, work email, company, approximate monthly application volume, and your message.
  • Contact page: your name, email, the topic you choose, and your message.

The expected-loss calculator and example decisions on the home page run entirely in your browser. The values you enter there are not sent to us or anyone else.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry and to discuss a possible backtest or pilot. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Service providers

  • Formspree receives form submissions and forwards them to us by email. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface used on this site, so your browser contacts Google's servers when the page loads. See Google's privacy policy.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as needed to respond and follow up. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, send a request through our contact page with the topic "Privacy request".

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
